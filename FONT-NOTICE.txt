The SIL Open Font License 1.1 is in OFL.txt.
Source revision and input SHA-256 checksums are in the separately distributed
release-manifest.json. Source file URLs are in sources/noto.lock.json in the
Noto Bene source repository.

Changes: renamed the family to Noto Bene; preserved ASCII outlines and advances;
added a circled exclamation mark of fixed 0.5 em diameter at the upper right of
each non-ASCII glyph; removed overlapping source ink with a circular Boolean
difference and 12% radial clearance around the badge, closing the cut contours;
expanded positive non-ASCII advances where necessary to include the final
right ink boundary plus 0.05 em, retained zero advances, and recomputed left
side bearings; used the original .notdef with the same badge and width rules
for otherwise unmapped non-ASCII Unicode scalar values; converted the original
.notdef without a badge or metric changes; converted cubic outlines to
quadratic TrueType outlines; removed original shaping tables. Blank non-ASCII
glyphs contain only the badge.
